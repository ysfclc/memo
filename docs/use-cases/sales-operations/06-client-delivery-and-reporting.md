# Use Case 06 — Client Delivery, Quotation Recovery and Reporting

**Actor:** Yusuf as service provider; client as data owner / business decision-maker  
**Outcome:** Deliver a useful operational improvement without overstepping client authority or exposing confidential information.

## Start-of-engagement checklist

- Confirm signed scope and payment milestone.
- Confirm named client sponsor and delivery contact.
- Confirm which CRM, spreadsheet, inbox or records are in scope.
- Agree permitted access and least-privilege accounts.
- Confirm confidentiality and data-processing requirements.
- Confirm whether client data may be processed locally or by any remote AI provider.
- Agree baseline measures and acceptance criteria.
- Agree retention/deletion and handover arrangements.

## Workflow A — Current-state audit

1. Collect process description from client.
2. Inspect only the agreed systems/records.
3. Map enquiry → qualification → quotation → follow-up → decision → order/lost.
4. Identify missing owners, stages, dates, next actions and loss reasons.
5. Use a client-approved sample. Do not copy an entire CRM by default.
6. Produce an issue list with evidence and severity.
7. Review findings with client before changing their system.

## Workflow B — Quotation recovery queue

1. Identify quotations that client has authorised for review.
2. Group by status: sent/no response, awaiting technical answer, revision requested, decision pending, expired, won, lost, unclear.
3. Detect missing owner or next action.
4. Suggest follow-up timing based on client rules, not an invented universal cadence.
5. Draft context-aware messages for the client's team to review.
6. Do not send customer messages or change quote terms without approval.
7. Record result and next action.
8. Flag sensitive disputes, pricing commitments or contractual matters to the client.

## Workflow C — CRM / pipeline configuration

1. Inspect existing CRM first; do not replace it unnecessarily.
2. Propose fields, stages and reminders.
3. Show a preview and migration plan.
4. Obtain approval before modifying records, automations or permissions.
5. Apply changes in a test/sandbox area if available.
6. Verify postconditions and provide rollback instructions.
7. Train the client owner and hand over documentation.

## Workflow D — Weekly review

Generate:
- New enquiries and their current status.
- Open quotations by stage and age.
- Quotes without an owner or next action.
- Follow-ups due / overdue.
- Waiting-on-client vs waiting-on-company categories.
- Won/lost outcomes where recorded.
- Missing data and process exceptions.
- Actions for the coming week.

Do not fabricate conversion rates or lost revenue. If baseline data is missing, state that a reliable measure is not yet possible.

## Client data protection

- Client remains controller where it determines purposes and means; Yusuf/Memo's role must be assessed for each engagement.
- Use least privilege and client-owned accounts.
- No reusing client contacts for Yusuf's own prospecting.
- No uploading client CRM data to remote models without explicit contractual permission and an appropriate data-processing basis.
- Avoid personal/sensitive data where not required.
- Maintain access logs and a clear deletion/return process.
- Do not retain credentials in notes or logs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Work stays within the signed scope.
- Every material change is approved and logged.
- Client-facing communication is approved by the client or Yusuf as agreed.
- The client can inspect deliverables and source data.
- Handover includes configuration notes, ownership and rollback/exit instructions.
- Report separates measured results, client-reported outcomes and hypotheses.
