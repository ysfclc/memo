# Use Case 05 — Discovery, Proposal and Client Agreement

**Actor:** Yusuf  
**Outcome:** Convert a confirmed operational problem into a small, understandable paid engagement.

## Discovery preparation

Memo creates a brief from account and opportunity records:
- Company and business context.
- Confirmed problem in prospect's words.
- Current workflow and tools.
- Existing baseline data, if supplied.
- Decision-makers and participants.
- Questions still unanswered.
- Scope risks and data-access constraints.

## Discovery meeting

Memo may:
- Prepare agenda and questions.
- Take notes from user-entered notes or an explicitly enabled recording/transcription feature after appropriate notice and consent.
- Separate client statements, Yusuf's observations and Memo's hypotheses.
- Summarise desired outcome and constraints.
- Draft a recap email for approval.

Memo must not record a meeting secretly or upload audio/transcripts to a remote model without explicit permission and disclosure.

## Pilot offer template

**Working title:** Sales Pipeline & Quotation Recovery Sprint.

**Example scope:**
- One business unit / sales team.
- One existing enquiry-to-quotation pipeline.
- Agreed sample of active or dormant quotations.
- Current process map.
- Pipeline stages and minimum required fields.
- Follow-up ownership and task queue.
- Lightweight CRM/spreadsheet configuration.
- Dashboard / weekly review view.
- Handover notes and team walkthrough.

**Out of scope unless separately agreed:**
- Guaranteeing orders, revenue or conversion rates.
- Purchasing contact lists or running mass cold campaigns.
- Replacing ERP/CRM platforms.
- Custom software development.
- Legal, tax or accounting advice.
- Managing the client's sales team.
- Access to unrelated personal/customer data.

**Commercial assumptions:** A 10-working-day, 8–12-hour pilot and PLN 900–1,500 gross test price may be explored with buyers, but are not fixed policy or market evidence. Memo must calculate actual hours, applicable minimum remuneration rules and tax treatment before a quote is finalised.

## Proposal generation

Memo drafts:
1. Client's confirmed situation.
2. Agreed objective.
3. Scope and deliverables.
4. Client responsibilities and access.
5. Timeline and milestones.
6. Fee, tax wording and payment schedule (human/accountant reviewed).
7. Acceptance criteria and revision boundary.
8. Confidentiality, data handling and any required data-processing terms.
9. Out-of-scope items and change process.
10. No guarantee of commercial outcome.
11. Termination/cancellation and dispute terms, reviewed by a qualified professional where needed.

## Contract-form decision

Memo must not automatically choose between Polish umowa zlecenie, umowa o świadczenie usług, umowa o dzieło, B2B invoice or foreign-client contract. Classification depends on actual work, operator's residence/work rights, legal status, client location, tax residence, ZUS coordination and whether the arrangement is truly a result-based work contract or ongoing service.

The system may prepare a factual summary and a checklist for an accountant/lawyer, but it must label the legal form “to be confirmed” until reviewed. Do not mislabel recurring sales support, CRM maintenance or ongoing prospecting as umowa o dzieło merely to avoid ZUS.

## Approval gates

- Yusuf approves scope and price.
- Client accepts the proposal.
- Yusuf or qualified signatory signs the final agreement.
- Memo never signs or accepts terms autonomously.
- No work starts until required contract, access and data permissions are confirmed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Proposal scope is traceable to discovery notes.
- Every price and legal/tax statement is labelled draft until confirmed.
- No guaranteed sales outcome is inserted by default.
- No client data access begins before agreed permissions and safeguards.
- Final proposal and signed agreement are stored as versioned artefacts.
