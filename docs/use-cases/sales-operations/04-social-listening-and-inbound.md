# Use Case 04 — Social Listening, Content and Inbound Demand

**Actor:** Yusuf  
**Outcome:** Use Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and other relevant channels to understand the market, demonstrate expertise and handle inbound interest.

## Product goal

Social channels are not simply outbound delivery pipes. Memo should help Yusuf identify real business conversations, learn the language industrial owners use, publish useful material, and handle requests that come to him.

## Listening workflow

1. Yusuf selects a segment or topic: CNC subcontracting, RFQs, quotation follow-up, injection moulding, tooling, industrial distribution, export sales, CRM adoption.
2. Memo searches only permitted public sources or connected accounts within granted permissions.
3. It collects relevant public business signals: a company announces a new machine, asks for suppliers, attends a trade fair, posts a new product line, discusses export expansion, or explicitly asks for help.
4. Each signal stores URL/source, timestamp, platform, exact public observation and confidence.
5. Memo classifies signal as:
   - Relevant market insight.
   - Possible account signal.
   - Inbound enquiry / request.
   - Content idea.
   - Irrelevant / low confidence.
6. It links an account only when identity is verified.
7. It suggests a next action. It does not automatically DM the author.

## Facebook

Potential uses:
- Identify relevant local industrial and manufacturing groups.
- Observe public discussions and public business-page updates within platform rules.
- Learn recurring pain points and terminology.
- Monitor connected Page comments/messages only when the account and API permissions support it.
- Draft helpful educational posts for Yusuf's own Page/profile or an authorised group, with human review and group-rule check.

Not allowed:
- Harvesting member lists or personal profiles at scale.
- Automated unsolicited Messenger campaigns.
- Posting identical promotional content across groups.
- Joining groups or impersonating customers to bypass restrictions.

## Instagram

Potential uses:
- Review public business profiles for capabilities, products, machinery and positioning.
- Monitor connected professional account interactions where official API permissions allow.
- Draft short educational content, carousels or Reels scripts explaining quotation management and sales process basics.
- Triage inbound comments and messages where available.

Not assumed:
- Access to all personal inboxes, Stories, private accounts or arbitrary user data.
- Ability to DM any account.
- Permission to scrape or automate consumer Instagram sessions.

## LinkedIn

Potential uses:
- Track public company updates and professional content through permitted access.
- Find relevant industry topics and event announcements.
- Draft thoughtful posts and comments for Yusuf to publish.
- Triage inbound interest and warm introductions.

Not assumed:
- Bulk profile extraction.
- Automated connection requests or DMs.
- Broad API access to personal messages or arbitrary profile data.

## YouTube and other content sources

Potential uses:
- Review company videos, product demonstrations, interviews and public webinars where accessible.
- Extract business facts and content themes with source links.
- Prepare short summaries and discovery questions.
- Never represent transcript-derived assumptions as confirmed company facts without verification.

## Content production workflow

1. Memo proposes a weekly theme based on actual research and discovery conversations.
2. Draft one useful post aimed at industrial owners, e.g. “What happens to a quotation after it leaves your inbox?”
3. Include a practical example or checklist; do not expose client confidential information.
4. Generate channel-specific versions: LinkedIn text, Facebook Page post, Instagram carousel outline, short video script.
5. Preserve the same factual core but adapt length and format; no deceptive fake testimonials or invented case studies.
6. Yusuf edits and approves each platform-specific publication.
7. After publication, record URL/time and any available engagement metrics.
8. Summarise meaningful inbound conversations and follow-up requests.

## Inbound response workflow

1. Detect an inbound message/comment only where connected permissions provide it.
2. Classify: genuine enquiry, referral, general question, spam, complaint, support request or unrelated.
3. Summarise context and suggest a response.
4. If the message asks for pricing, contract, legal assurance or a commitment, flag for Yusuf rather than invent an answer.
5. Require approval before sending a reply.
6. Link the conversation to an account/opportunity only after identity is verified and appropriate.
7. Capture opt-out, objection or privacy request immediately.

## Acceptance criteria

- Signals include platform, URL, date and evidence.
- Content is based on verified knowledge or clearly labelled opinion.
- Client confidential data is never reused in public content without permission.
- Inbound replies are not confused with permission for unrelated marketing.
- Posting and replies require exact-action approval.
- The UI clearly distinguishes “public source research”, “connected account data” and “manual user-provided context”.
