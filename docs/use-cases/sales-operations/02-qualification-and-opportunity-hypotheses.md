# Use Case 02 — Qualification and Commercial Problem Hypotheses

**Actor:** Yusuf  
**Outcome:** Decide whether a company has a problem worth discussing before spending time pitching.

## Trigger

An account has passed initial research, a referrer offers an introduction, a company responds inbound, or Yusuf meets a decision-maker.

## Qualification dimensions

Memo should distinguish observable facts from interview answers and assumptions.

1. **Business fit:** B2B industrial offer, target segments, relevant geography and customer type.
2. **Existing commercial activity:** inbound enquiries, RFQs, quotations, repeat orders, distributors or export sales.
3. **Process friction:** no clear quote owner, manual tracking, overdue follow-ups, scattered inboxes, no visibility of dormant quotes, weak handover or poor CRM adoption.
4. **Ability to act:** someone owns the process, access to a sample of records can be granted, team can participate, no major IT project required.
5. **Buying readiness:** decision-maker access, urgency, willingness to pay for a limited diagnostic/pilot.
6. **Access and trust:** warm introduction, explicit inbound request or other reviewed lawful communication route.
7. **Delivery fit:** the problem is within Yusuf's competence and can be scoped in a small engagement.

## Discovery conversation flow

Memo prepares a one-page call brief:
- What the company makes/sells.
- Why it appears relevant.
- What is known and unknown.
- Three to six open questions.
- One or two problem hypotheses, never presented as facts.
- Suggested next step if fit is confirmed.

Suggested questions:
- “How does a new RFQ enter the company and who owns it?”
- “Where do you track quotations after they are sent?”
- “How do you know which open quotations need follow-up this week?”
- “What happens when the person responsible is busy or absent?”
- “Do you have a way to see why quotations are lost?”
- “If we improved one part of this process in two weeks, which would matter most?”

Memo should avoid leading the prospect into a problem they have not confirmed.

## Qualification outcomes

- **Qualified:** clear process issue, owner, access and plausible budget; prepare pilot scope.
- **Nurture:** fit exists but timing or access is not ready; record a consent-appropriate next step.
- **Disqualified:** no fit, no need, no authority/access, prohibited contact route, or unreasonable expectations.
- **Needs discovery:** evidence is insufficient; do not invent a probability.

## Opportunity record

Capture:
- Confirmed problem in the prospect's own words (short quote only if appropriate).
- Current process and tools.
- Baseline if the client voluntarily supplies it (e.g. number of open quotations).
- Impact described by client; do not invent monetary loss.
- Decision-maker and other stakeholders.
- Timing and urgency.
- Data access and confidentiality restrictions.
- Agreed next step and date.
- Risks, objections and unanswered questions.

## Acceptance criteria

- Memo never marks a prospect “qualified” based solely on company size or industry.
- All assumptions are visibly labelled.
- No probability, budget or revenue estimate is presented as fact without evidence.
- Yusuf can edit every note and qualification outcome.
- The next action is clear and proportionate.
